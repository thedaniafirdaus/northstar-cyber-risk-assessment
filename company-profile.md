# Northstar Digital Ltd — Company Profile

**Scenario:** Fictional UK business created for
an independent GRC portfolio assessment.

## Business Overview
- Industry: Digital services and software
- Location: United Kingdom
- Employees: 75
- Working arrangement: Hybrid and remote
- Technology: Microsoft 365, employee laptops,
  cloud applications
- Information handled: Customer records,
  employee data, commercial documents
- Emerging technology: Generative AI tools

## Assessment Objectives
1. Identify critical information assets.
2. Assess cybersecurity risks and business impact.
3. Evaluate assumed controls and identify gaps.
4. Map findings to NIST CSF 2.0 and selected
   ISO 27001 controls.
5. Prioritise risk treatment and report
   recommendations to management.

## Assessment Boundaries
This is a simulated desktop assessment.
No real company systems, employees or customer
records are accessed. Control findings are not
verified audit evidence.
