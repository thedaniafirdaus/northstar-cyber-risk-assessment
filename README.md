# Northstar Digital Ltd — Cyber Risk & Compliance Assessment

**Independent GRC Portfolio Project | Cyber Risk | Information Security | Security Assurance**

**Prepared by:** Dania Firdaus

## Project Overview

This project presents a simulated cybersecurity risk and compliance assessment for Northstar Digital Ltd, a fictional UK digital services company with approximately 75 employees.

Northstar uses Microsoft 365, employee laptops, cloud services and customer information systems. The assessment explores how a Cyber Risk Analyst might identify security risks, evaluate control evidence, prioritise remediation and communicate findings to management.

All business scenarios, scores and findings are fictional. No real organisation was audited.

## Assessment Scope

The project covers:

- Asset identification and classification
- Threat and vulnerability analysis
- Cybersecurity risk identification and scoring
- Inherent and residual risk concepts
- Control evidence assessment
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 mapping
- Selected ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A control mapping
- UK Cyber Essentials gap assessment
- Risk treatment planning
- Executive risk reporting and risk visualisation

## Key Findings

The assessment identified ten fictional inherent risk scenarios:

- **2 Critical risks**
- **8 High risks**

The highest-scoring scenarios were customer database compromise and ransomware, each scoring 20 out of 25.

Six risks were assigned proposed P1 treatment priority and four were assigned P2 priority.

These are illustrative priorities and scores, not verified organisational findings.

## Risk Assessment Methodology

Risks were assessed using:

**Inherent Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**

Both likelihood and impact use a five-point scale.

| Score | Rating |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Low |
| 6–11 | Medium |
| 12–19 | High |
| 20–25 | Critical |

Residual risk remains unassessed where implemented control effectiveness has not been demonstrated.

## Frameworks Used

**NIST CSF 2.0:** Selected categories across cybersecurity governance, identification, protection, detection and recovery.

**ISO/IEC 27001:2022:** Selected Annex A controls relevant to access management, endpoint security, malware protection, backup security and information protection.

**UK Cyber Essentials:** Preliminary assessment across firewalls, secure configuration, security update management, user access control and malware protection.

Framework mappings demonstrate relevance, not compliance certification.

## Priority Recommendations

1. Strengthen identity and access management.
2. Verify endpoint encryption and security controls.
3. Improve ransomware protection and patch management.
4. Test backup restoration and business recovery.
5. Review privileged user permissions.
6. Establish clearer AI governance and data-handling requirements.

## Limitations

Northstar Digital Ltd is fictional. All risks, findings, evidence scenarios, owners and treatment priorities were developed for educational purposes.

No real infrastructure was tested, and no ISO 27001 or Cyber Essentials certification assessment was performed.

This project demonstrates an independent GRC assessment workflow rather than commercial audit experience.
