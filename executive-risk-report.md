# Northstar Digital Ltd
## Executive Cybersecurity Risk Assessment Report

**Prepared by:** Dania Firdaus  
**Assessment type:** Independent simulated GRC portfolio project  
**Organisation:** Northstar Digital Ltd (fictional)  
**Assessment status:** Preliminary desktop assessment

---

### 1. Executive Summary

Northstar Digital Ltd is a fictional UK digital services company employing approximately 75 people. The organisation uses Microsoft 365, employee laptops, cloud applications and customer information systems.

This assessment identifies 10 cybersecurity risk scenarios that could affect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of business information.

Based on the illustrative inherent risk scoring methodology:

- **2 risks are classified as Critical.**
- **8 risks are classified as High.**
- **6 risks have been assigned P1 treatment priority.**
- **4 risks have been assigned P2 treatment priority.**

The highest-scoring scenarios involve customer database compromise and ransomware.

The assessment is based on fictional business assumptions and simulated evidence. It does not establish that Northstar has experienced these incidents or that its security controls have failed.

### 3. Control Assurance Findings

A simulated evidence assessment was prepared across 10 risk scenarios.

The laptop encryption exercise assumes that 54 of 60 devices report encryption, while six remain unverified.

This suggests incomplete assurance over encryption coverage in the fictional scenario.

For the remaining risks, the assessment documents the evidence required to evaluate security controls. Evidence has not been supplied or independently verified.

Consequently, the effectiveness of the proposed controls cannot yet be established.

### 4. Recommended Priority Actions

**P1 — Priority Treatment**

Northstar should prioritise the following proposed actions:

1. Strengthen customer database authentication and access controls.
2. Verify full-disk encryption across in-scope company laptops.
3. Strengthen Microsoft 365 authentication and sign-in monitoring.
4. Improve endpoint protection and security update management.
5. Validate backup protection and restoration procedures.
6. Review privileged accounts and remove unnecessary access.

The proposed target for these actions is 14 days, subject to management agreement, resourcing and technical feasibility.

**P2 — Planned Remediation**

The remaining actions cover HR access permissions, AI governance, website security and email fraud prevention.

The proposed target is 30 days, subject to management agreement.

These priorities should be reviewed against business requirements, current controls and actual threat exposure.

### 5. Framework Alignment

The assessment includes preliminary mapping to:

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A
- UK Cyber Essentials technical control areas

These mappings identify potentially relevant security controls and evidence requirements.

They do not demonstrate certification, regulatory compliance or successful implementation.

### 6. Residual Risk

Residual risk has not been formally assessed because the necessary evidence of implemented and effective controls is unavailable.

Residual risk scores should be established after relevant controls have been implemented, tested and evaluated.

### 7. Management Decisions Required

For this fictional exercise, management would be asked to:

1. Review and approve the proposed risk treatment priorities.
2. Assign accountable owners and confirm remediation deadlines.
3. Authorise collection and review of relevant security evidence.
4. Agree risk appetite and escalation thresholds.
5. Review residual risk after control verification.

### 8. Assessment Limitations

This report is based entirely on a fictional company and simulated desktop assessment.

No real corporate systems were accessed, no actual controls were independently tested, and no real audit evidence was collected.

The results demonstrate a structured GRC assessment methodology rather than a verified security assessment.

### 9. Conclusion

Northstar Digital Ltd's fictional risk assessment highlights the importance of identity security, endpoint protection, information access management and incident recovery.

The recommended next step is to validate security control effectiveness, address priority gaps and reassess residual risk.

This assessment demonstrates the practical process of identifying risks, documenting evidence requirements, mapping security frameworks and communicating recommendations to management.
