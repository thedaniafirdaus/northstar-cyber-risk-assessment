# Northstar Digital Ltd — Cyber Risk Assessment Methodology

## Purpose
This document explains how cybersecurity risks are
identified, assessed and prioritised.

## Risk Identification
Each risk identifies an asset, threat, assumed
vulnerability and potential business consequences.

## Likelihood Scale
1 — Rare: Highly exceptional.
2 — Unlikely: Could occur but is not expected.
3 — Possible: Credible event that may occur.
4 — Likely: Expected under assumed conditions.
5 — Almost Certain: Expected frequently or imminently.

## Impact Scale
1 — Negligible: Minimal disruption.
2 — Minor: Limited consequences.
3 — Moderate: Noticeable disruption or loss.
4 — Major: Significant business consequences.
5 — Severe: Extremely serious consequences.

## Inherent Risk Scoring
Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

- Low: 1–5
- Medium: 6–11
- High: 12–19
- Critical: 20–25

## Assessment Assumptions
Scores are illustrative and based on fictional
scenarios, not verified organisational evidence.

## Risk Treatment
Risks may be reduced, avoided, transferred or
accepted, subject to management approval.

## Residual Risk
Residual risk is assessed after considering
implemented controls and their effectiveness.
Proposed controls do not automatically reduce risk.

## Review
Reassess risks when threats, systems, controls
or business circumstances change.
